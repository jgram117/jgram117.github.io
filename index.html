Projects

Difference-in-Differences

The Effect of the Universal DH Rule on National League Offense

Estimated the causal effect of MLB's 2022 universal designated hitter rule on National League offensive output using a difference-in-differences design. Found a statistically significant average treatment effect of approximately +0.31 runs per game (p=0.022).

Difference-in-Differences

The Kalamazoo Promise and Housing Costs

Examined the effect of the Kalamazoo Promise scholarship program on local housing costs using American Community Survey microdata and a difference-in-differences design with two-way fixed effects in Python.

Industrial Organization  |  Stackelberg Oligopoly

The AI Chip Industry: Market Structure and Competition

Analyzed the AI chip industry as a Stackelberg oligopoly with Nvidia as the dominant leader. Examined barriers to entry, vertical supply chain concentration (Nvidia, TSMC, ASML), welfare implications of market power, pricing dynamics, and the structural effects of the CHIPS Act and U.S. export controls on global semiconductor competition.
